WHY IBADAN?

A city of hills, heritage, and character.

Ibadan is one of Nigeria's historic cities and the capital of Oyo State. Its landscape combines hills, traditional communities, historic buildings, educational institutions, markets, gardens, and cultural spaces. Explore this guide to discover places that reveal different sides of the city.
