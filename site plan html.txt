<h2>Site Name</h2>
<p><strong>Explore Nigeria Travel Guide</strong></p>
<p>
The site name was selected because it clearly communicates the purpose
of helping visitors discover tourist attractions, culture, and travel
destinations across Nigeria.
</p>

<section>
<h2>Site Purpose</h2>
<p>
This website will provide information about popular tourist destinations,
cultural attractions, travel tips, and local events throughout Nigeria.
Visitors can learn about places to visit and plan future trips.
</p>
</section>

</ul>
