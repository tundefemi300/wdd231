Site Name

Lagos Tech Hub

Lagos Tech Hub is a website designed to connect students, developers, entrepreneurs, and technology enthusiasts with technology events, training programs, and career opportunities across Lagos, Nigeria.

Suggested Domain: lagostechhub.org

Site Purpose

The website provides information about technology events, coding bootcamps, workshops, and networking opportunities. Visitors can browse upcoming events, read about organizers, contact the team, and register for programs.

Scenarios

  • Where can I find upcoming technology events in Lagos?
  • How do I register for a coding bootcamp or workshop?
  • How can I contact the event organizers?

Color Scheme

  • #0F4C81 (Blue) – Header, navigation, buttons, headings
  • #F4F4F4 (Light Gray) – Background
  • #2E8B57 (Green) – Highlights and call-to-action buttons
  • #333333 (Dark Gray) – Body text

Typography

  • Poppins – Headings
  • Roboto – Body text
